# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project respectful and free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual
identity or orientation, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge different experience levels.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and safety constraints.
- Accept proportionate moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, impersonation, spam, malicious code, unsafe instructions,
or publishing another person's private or proprietary information is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact
method added during the publishing checklist. Do not include sensitive evidence
in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions,
issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a person,
depending on context, severity, pattern, and safety. Good-faith reports and
appeals will be handled as confidentially and impartially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person is publicly representing
the project. It does not create an employment, agency, support, or contractual
relationship.
