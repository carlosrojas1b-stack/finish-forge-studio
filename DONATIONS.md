# Donations

Donations are not currently configured. `.github/FUNDING.yml` contains commented
examples that a maintainer can enable later.

If donations are enabled:

- Contributions are voluntary support for project maintenance.
- A donation does not purchase software, support, priority, influence, equity,
  ownership, a warranty, indemnity, certification, or a particular feature.
- The software remains available under AGPL-3.0-or-later regardless
  of donation status.
- Donations are not represented as charitable or tax-deductible unless a
  qualified entity explicitly states otherwise. Donors should obtain their own
  tax advice.
- Payment-platform fees, privacy practices, currencies, chargebacks, refunds,
  sanctions, age limits, and regional restrictions are governed by the platform
  and applicable law.
- Unless required by law or the platform's rules, donations should be treated as
  non-refundable after processing. Resolve mistakes through the platform without
  posting financial details publicly.
- Maintainers should disclose sponsorships that could reasonably create a
  perceived conflict and should never accept funds conditioned on inserting
  malicious, deceptive, infringing, or unsafe behavior.

Before enabling a button, update `FUNDING.yml`, this policy, the privacy
statement, and the README; verify account ownership; and obtain accounting or
legal advice if donations become material or recurring.
