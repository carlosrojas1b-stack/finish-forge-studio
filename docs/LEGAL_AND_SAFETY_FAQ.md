# Legal and safety FAQ

## Is the project open source?

Yes. Original project code is under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party software keeps
its own license, and the project license does not cover third-party trademarks,
standards text, proprietary textures, or user data.

## Can it be used commercially?

The AGPL permits commercial use of covered code, subject to its strong copyleft
and source-offer requirements. It does not prohibit corporate use. It does make
covered proprietary forks and undisclosed modified network deployments harder
by requiring corresponding source under the same license. That does not decide
whether a specific input, texture, brand, product, output, standard, patent,
regulation, contract, or third-party dependency permits the planned activity.
Obtain professional advice for the actual use.

## Are MT, SPI/SPE, or VDI results official?

No. They are reference-oriented synthesized previews and metadata. The project
is not affiliated with or certified by the relevant organizations or suppliers.

## Can proprietary texture scans be contributed?

Only if the contributor holds clear redistribution rights compatible with the
repository license and documents those rights. Customer data, confidential
drawings, licensed standards, and unauthorized plaque scans should not be posted.

## Does local-first mean perfectly confidential?

No. It removes an intentional application upload path in the supplied build, but
the browser, extensions, operating system, hosting, dependencies, malware, or a
modified fork can still affect confidentiality. Review the deployment.

## Is a 25 µm profile dimensionally accurate to 25 µm?

No. Layer height is not overall accuracy or XY feature resolution. Printed
results require process-specific measurement and compensation.

## Can the output be used for safety-critical products?

Not without an independent qualified engineering, verification, validation,
risk-management, and regulatory program. The project itself is not certified.

## Does a donation buy support or features?

No. Donations, if enabled, are voluntary and do not create a warranty, service
level, roadmap commitment, ownership interest, or tax representation.
