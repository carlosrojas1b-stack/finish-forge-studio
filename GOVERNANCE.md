# Governance

Finish Forge Studio currently uses a maintainer-led model. The repository owner
sets releases, roadmap, scope, moderation, merge criteria, and security response,
while considering community input. A proposal or contribution does not guarantee
acceptance.

## Decision principles

Maintainers should prioritize user safety, correct disclosure of approximation,
local-first processing, reproducible behavior, maintainability, license
compliance, and practical value for prototype workflows. Decisions may also
consider review capacity and long-term support cost.

## Conflicts and funding

Maintainers and reviewers should disclose financial, employment, supplier, or
other interests that could reasonably affect a decision. Donations do not buy
votes, roadmap control, endorsement, or privileged access. Material sponsorships
should be disclosed publicly with any conditions.

## Changes to governance

Governance may evolve as the maintainer group grows. Material changes should be
documented in the repository. No governance document creates a partnership,
joint venture, fiduciary relationship, employment relationship, or contractual
right to continued maintenance.
