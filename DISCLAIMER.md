## Geometry and dimensional accuracy

STEP geometry is tessellated and texture relief alters mesh geometry. Outputs
may contain approximation error, non-manifold edges, self-intersections, thin
walls, holes, inverted normals, degenerate triangles, excessive polygon counts,
or loss of detail. Depth, spacing, rotation, boundary fading, tessellation,
resampling, printer compensation, slicing, orientation, cure, shrinkage,
post-processing, and material behavior can change final dimensions.

Never assume an exported STL or 3MF preserves nominal CAD dimensions or fits.
Measure printed coupons and parts. Protect threads, seals, bearing seats,
electrical interfaces, snap fits, optical faces, datum features, and mating or
critical-tolerance surfaces unless a qualified engineer approves otherwise.
